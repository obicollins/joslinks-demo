How information is handled in this JosLinks website preview.

JL — PEOPLE IN MOTION

About this preview

This website is a demonstration of the JosLinks digital experience. Its contact email, phone number, office location and business hours are illustrative. This page describes the preview’s behaviour and should be reviewed and replaced with an approved organisation privacy notice before a public launch.

The enquiry form

The form uses the details you enter to prepare an enquiry within your browser. It does not send your information to a JosLinks server or save it in a recruitment or customer database. You may download or copy your enquiry, or choose to open a draft in your own email application. Your email provider handles any draft or message you choose to create.

Information to leave out

Please do not enter identity numbers, financial details, health information, employee records or other sensitive personal information. A short description of your question is enough for this demonstration.

Storage and site services

This website does not set its own analytics or advertising cookies. Fonts and the workplace image are served with the site. The hosting provider may process technical request information needed to serve and protect the website, and private-preview access is managed by the hosting platform.

Before public use

The organisation should verify its service information, approve its privacy wording, add a monitored contact channel, and confirm how enquiries will be received, secured, retained and deleted. This preview does not establish those operational arrangements.

Illustrative visuals

The site uses original vector graphics, geometric illustrations and icons. These visuals express connection and possibility; they do not depict actual JosLinks employees, clients or offices. The articles provide general discussion points and are not professional advice.
